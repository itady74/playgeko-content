## Step 1: Define Your League

### League Format

**Round robin.** Everyone plays everyone in their group. Best for small leagues.

**Ladder league.** Players challenge up the ladder. Flexible scheduling.

**Division league.** Skill-based divisions. Fair competition.

**Season league.** Fixed duration with playoffs. Creates excitement.

### League Structure

**Individual.** Players compete individually.

**Doubles.** Partner-based competition.

**Mixed.** Mixed doubles format.

**Team.** Teams of multiple players.

### Duration

**Short.** 4-6 weeks. Quick, intense.

**Medium.** 8-12 weeks. Standard season.

**Long.** 16-24 weeks. Extended season.

## Step 2: Set Rules

### Match Rules

**Scoring.** Standard padel scoring (15, 30, 40, deuce).

**Tiebreak.** First to 7 points at 6-6.

**Sets.** Best of 1 or best of 3.

**Match duration.** Time-based or set-based.

### League Rules

**Eligibility.** Who can join? Skill level requirements?

**Schedule.** When are matches played?

**Rescheduling.** How to handle conflicts.

**Forfeits.** What happens when someone doesn't show?

**Disputes.** How to resolve disagreements.

### Rankings

**Points system.** How points are awarded.

**Tiebreakers.** How ties are broken.

**Promotion/relegation.** Movement between divisions.

## Step 3: Registration

### Registration Process

**Online registration.** Use PlayGeko or similar platform.

**Information required:**
- Player name and contact
- Skill level (self-assessed or verified)
- Partner name (for doubles)
- Preferred schedule
- Payment

### Payment

**Entry fee.** $20-100 per player (market dependent).

**Payment methods.** Online, bank transfer, cash.

**Refund policy.** Clear terms for cancellations.

### Team Formation

**Individual registration.** Players register solo, organizers pair them.

**Team registration.** Pre-formed teams register together.

**Waitlist.** When full, maintain a waitlist.

### Schedule Creation

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Too Complex

Keep rules simple. Complexity confuses players and creates disputes.

### Mistake 2: Poor Communication

Players don't know when/where to play. Results aren't posted. Keep everyone informed.

### Mistake 3: No Flexibility

Life happens. Allow reasonable rescheduling.

### Mistake 4: Ignoring Community

Leagues are about competition and community. Build both.

### Mistake 5: No Follow-Up

League ends, no results published, no photos shared. Missed community-building opportunity.

## FAQ

### How many players do I need for a league?

Minimum 8 players for a meaningful league. 16-32 is ideal for good competition.

### How long should a league last?

4-12 weeks is typical. Shorter leagues create urgency. Longer leagues build community.

### What's the best format for beginners?

Round robin or social league formats. Less pressure, more fun.

### How do I handle disputes?

Define a clear dispute resolution process. Designate a league director to make final decisions.
