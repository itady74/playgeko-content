## Core AI Features

### Demand Forecasting

**What it does:** Predicts which hours, days, and courts will be busy — and which won't.

**How it works:** AI analyzes historical booking data, weather patterns, seasonal trends, and local events to forecast demand.

**What you get:**
- **Staff optimization:** Staff more during predicted busy periods. Reduce hours during slow times.
- **Court maintenance:** Schedule maintenance during predicted low-demand windows
- **Event planning:** Know when to host tournaments, clinics, and social events
- **Pricing adjustments:** Dynamic pricing based on predicted demand

**Real example:** A padel club noticed Tuesday evenings were consistently underbooked. AI recommended a 20% discount for Tuesday bookings. Occupancy increased 35% within two months.

### Smart Pricing Recommendations

**What it does:** Suggests pricing adjustments based on demand patterns, competitor pricing, and member behavior.

**How it works:** AI analyzes your pricing against occupancy rates, member spending patterns, and market conditions.

**What you get:**
- **Peak pricing:** Increase rates during high-demand periods
- **Off-peak discounts:** Reduce rates during slow periods to fill courts
- **Member retention pricing:** Special rates for at-risk members
- **Coaching package optimization:** Price packages based on demand and value

**Real example:** A tennis club's AI recommended raising Friday evening rates by 15% and lowering Monday morning rates by 25%. Revenue increased 12% with no change in total bookings.

### Churn Prediction

**What it does:** Identifies members at risk of leaving before they do.

**How it works:** AI tracks booking frequency, spending patterns, and engagement signals. When behavior changes, it flags at-risk members.

**What you get:**
- **Early warnings:** Get alerted when a member's behavior changes
- **Automated re-engagement:** Trigger personalized emails or promotions
- **Retention offers:** Special discounts for members showing churn signals
- **Root cause analysis:** Understand why members are leaving

**Real example:** A multi-sport facility's AI flagged 15 members who hadn't booked in 21 days. Automated emails with 10% discount offers brought back 8 of them. Each retained member was worth $600+ annually.

### Upsell Recommendations

**What it does:** Suggests coaching packages, memberships, and add-ons to individual members.

**How it works:** AI analyzes each member's booking history, skill level, and spending to recommend relevant upgrades.

**What you get:**
- **Coaching suggestions:** Recommend lessons to players who would benefit
- **Membership upgrades:** Suggest premium tiers to active casual players
- **Package recommendations:** Offer lesson bundles based on frequency
- **Gear suggestions:** Recommend equipment based on skill level and preferences

**Real example:** A padel club's AI recommended coaching packages to players who had booked 5+ times but never taken a lesson. 12% conversion rate on recommendations, adding $15,000 in annual coaching revenue.

### Occupancy Optimization

**What it does:** Maximizes court utilization by filling empty slots.

**How it works:** AI identifies underutilized time slots and suggests promotions, open play sessions, or membership incentives.

**What you get:**
- **Empty slot alerts:** Know which courts are underperforming
- **Promotion targeting:** Target inactive members during slow periods
- **Open play scheduling:** Auto-schedule social sessions during low-demand windows
- **Waitlist optimization:** Fill cancellations instantly from waitlists

**Real example:** A squash club's AI identified that 30% of court time was unused during weekday mornings. Automated promotions to inactive members filled 60% of those slots within one month.

Yes. AI makes recommendations. You make decisions. Every recommendation can be accepted, modified, or rejected.

Yes. AI identifies target segments, suggests promotional timing, and recommends content based on member behavior and preferences.
