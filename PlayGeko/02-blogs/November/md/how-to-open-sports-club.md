## Introduction

Opening a sports club is exciting. It's also complex. Planning, financing, facility setup, staffing, marketing — there's a lot to coordinate.

This guide walks you through every step of opening a new sports club, from concept to launch.

---

## Step 1: Planning

### Define Your Concept

**Target market.** Who are you serving? Beginners? Competitive players? Families?

**Sports offered.** Padel, tennis, squash, basketball, multi-sport?

**Facility type.** Indoor, outdoor, or both?

**Price positioning.** Budget, mid-range, or premium?

### Market Research

**Competitor analysis.** What exists in your market? What's missing?

**Demand assessment.** Is there enough demand for another facility?

**Pricing research.** What do competitors charge?

**Location analysis.** Where are your target customers located?

### Business Plan

**Executive summary.** Your concept, market, and financials.

**Market analysis.** Your research findings.

**Operations plan.** How you'll run the club.

**Financial projections.** Revenue, expenses, and profitability.

**Marketing plan.** How you'll attract members.

---

## Step 2: Financing

### Startup Costs

**Facility costs.** Rent, purchase, or lease.

**Construction/renovation.** Building courts, installing equipment.

**Equipment.** Courts, nets, lighting, POS systems.

**Technology.** Club management software, website, app.

**Marketing.** Launch campaign, branding, materials.

**Working capital.** 6-12 months of operating expenses.

### Funding Options

**Personal savings.** Your own investment.

**Bank loans.** Traditional financing.

**Investors.** Partners or venture capital.

**SBA loans.** Government-backed loans.

**Equipment financing.** Lease equipment instead of buying.

### Financial Planning

**Break-even analysis.** When will you be profitable?

**Cash flow projection.** When will money come in and go out?

**Sensitivity analysis.** What if things don't go as planned?

## Step 3: Legal and Administrative

### Business Structure

**LLC.** Limited liability protection.

**Corporation.** More complex but additional benefits.

**Partnership.** If you have co-founders.

### Permits and Licenses

**Business license.** General operating license.

**Zoning permits.** Ensure your location allows sports facilities.

**Building permits.** For construction and renovation.

**Health permits.** If offering food and beverage.

### Insurance

**General liability.** Third-party injuries.

**Property insurance.** Your physical assets.

**Professional liability.** Coaching services.

**Workers' compensation.** Employee injuries.

### Contracts

**Lease agreements.** Facility terms.

**Vendor contracts.** Equipment and service providers.

**Employment agreements.** Staff terms.

**Member agreements.** Terms and conditions.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Underestimating Costs

Startup costs are always higher than expected. Add 20-30% buffer.

### Mistake 2: Rushing the Launch

Take time to get everything right. A bad launch is hard to recover from.

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Marketing

If you build it, they won't come unless you tell them about it.

### Mistake 4: No Differentiation

What makes you different from competitors? Define it clearly.

### Mistake 5: Poor Location

A great facility in a bad location struggles. Choose wisely.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to open a sports club?

Varies widely. Small facility: $100,000-300,000. Medium facility: $300,000-1,000,000. Large facility: $1,000,000+.

### How long does it take to open?

6-18 months depending on facility condition, construction needs, and permits.

### What's the most important factor?

Location. A great location with poor facilities can succeed. Poor location with great facilities struggles.

### Should I buy or lease?

Depends on your situation. Leasing is lower risk. Buying builds equity. Consider your long-term plans.
