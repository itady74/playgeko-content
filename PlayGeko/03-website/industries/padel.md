Padel isn't tennis. It isn't squash. The court layout, the scoring system, the rotation format, the coaching model — everything is different. Your management software should know that.

Generic club management tools treat padel as "tennis with walls." PlayGeko doesn't. Every feature is designed around how padel clubs actually operate:

- **4-player court dynamics:** Padel courts hold 4 players. PlayGeko handles 2v2 bookings, match rotation, and open match fill — not just single-court reservations.
- **Level-based matching:** Padel uses a 1-7 rating system. PlayGeko's dynamic leveling matches players by skill so matches are competitive and fun.
- **Glass wall logistics:** Padel courts have different surfaces (glass, mesh, turf). PlayGeko tracks court conditions and maintenance schedules per surface type.
- **Social format:** Padel is inherently social. PlayGeko supports group bookings, team events, and social leagues — not just 1v1 reservations.

## Core Features for Padel Clubs

### Court Booking for Padel

- **4-player court support:** Book pairs, not just courts. Two players book together, two more join as opponents.
- **Real-time availability:** Players see live court status. No phone calls. No WhatsApp messages.
- **Automated reminders:** Push and email notifications before matches reduce no-shows by up to 40%
- **Recurring bookings:** Members reserve their weekly Saturday morning slot — it auto-renews
- **Waitlist management:** When courts are full, players join a queue. They get notified when a slot opens.

**Typical scenario:** A group of 4 friends wants to play Thursday evening. One opens the app, books a court, and shares the link. The other 3 confirm. Payment is split. Court is booked. Reminder sent. Done.
