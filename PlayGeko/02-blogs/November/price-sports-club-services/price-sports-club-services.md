## Introduction

Pricing is the most powerful lever in your business. Set prices too high, players stay away. Set them too low, you leave money on the table.

Most clubs price based on gut feel or competitor copying. Neither is optimal.

This guide shows you how to price strategically.

---

## Pricing Framework

### Cost-Plus Pricing

**Calculate costs.** Determine cost per service unit.

**Add margin.** Add desired profit margin.

**Verify market.** Ensure pricing is competitive.

### Value-Based Pricing

**What's it worth to players?** Price based on perceived value.

**Premium experiences.** Charge more for superior service.

**Convenience premium.** Easy booking justifies higher prices.

### Competitive Pricing

**Market rates.** What do competitors charge?

**Positioning.** Where do you want to be? Budget, mid, premium?

**Differentiation.** What makes you worth more?

## Court Pricing

### Pricing Models

**Hourly.** Per court per hour.

**Session-based.** Fixed price for a session (e.g., 90 minutes).

**Per-player.** Individual pricing for court access.

**Dynamic.** Prices change based on demand.

### Peak/Off-Peak

**Peak hours.** Friday evening, Saturday morning, Sunday afternoon. Highest prices.

**Standard hours.** Weekday evenings, weekend afternoons. Standard prices.

**Off-peak hours.** Weekday mornings, late evenings. Lowest prices.

### Price Ratios

- **Off-peak:** 1.0x (base price)
- **Standard:** 1.3-1.5x
- **Peak:** 1.8-2.2x

## Membership Pricing

### Tiered Structure

**Basic.** Access to booking platform, marketplace listing.

**Premium.** Discounted court rates, priority booking, coaching discounts.

**All-access.** Unlimited booking, coaching discounts, tournament entry.

### Pricing Strategy

**Below per-booking cost.** Incentivize membership over casual play.

**Annual vs. monthly.** Annual discounts for commitment.

**Family plans.** Multi-person discounts.

### The Math

If a member books 4 times/month at $25/session, they spend $100/month. Offer a membership at $80/month. Member saves $20. You get predictable revenue.

## Coaching Pricing

### Session Types

**Individual.** 1-on-1 coaching. Highest price.

**Semi-private.** 2-4 players. Shared cost.

**Group clinic.** 4-8 players. Lowest cost per player.

### Package Pricing

**5-pack.** Popular for beginners.

**10-pack.** Committed players.

**Monthly unlimited.** Dedicated students.

**Discount:** 10-20% vs. individual pricing.

### Coach Differentiation

**Premium coaches.** Higher prices for more experienced coaches.

**Standard coaches.** Base prices.

**Junior coaches.** Lower prices for less experienced.

## Day Pass Pricing

### What Are Day Passes?

Single-session access for casual players, tourists, and visitors.

### Pricing Strategy

**Above membership rate.** 10-20% above equivalent per-session membership rate.

**Multi-visit passes.** Buy 5 or 10 at discount.

**Corporate passes.** Sell to businesses.

## Tournament and League Pricing

### Entry Fees

**Tournament entry.** $20-100 per team.

**League entry.** $30-150 per player for season.

**Social events.** $10-30 per person.

### Value Justification

**Prizes.** What winners receive.

**Experience.** Quality of organization.

**Community.** Social value of participation.

## Dynamic Pricing

### When to Use

**High demand.** Increase prices.

**Low demand.** Decrease prices.

**Weather-dependent.** Adjust for conditions.

**Seasonal.** Adjust for seasons.

### Implementation

**Set rules.** Define price adjustment triggers.

**Set limits.** Minimum and maximum prices.

**Communicate.** Tell players about dynamic pricing.

## Pricing Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Racing to the Bottom

Undercutting competitors destroys margins and signals low quality.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Costs

Price below cost and you lose money on every transaction.

### Mistake 3: No Differentiation

One price for everything leaves money on the table.

### Mistake 4: Complex Pricing

If players can't understand it in 10 seconds, it's too complicated.

### Mistake 5: Never Changing

Markets change. Prices should too.

## FAQ

### How do I know if my prices are right?

Track utilization and revenue. If courts are full, prices may be too low. If empty, too high. Gather player feedback.

### How often should I change prices?

Review quarterly. Adjust based on market conditions, costs, and demand patterns.

### Should I offer discounts?

Yes, strategically. Off-peak discounts fill empty courts. Membership discounts create predictable revenue.

### How do I communicate price changes?

Clearly and in advance. Explain the value. Give existing members advance notice.
