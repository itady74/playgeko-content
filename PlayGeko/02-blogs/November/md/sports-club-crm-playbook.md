## Introduction

CRM isn't just software. It's how you know, serve, and retain your members.

Most clubs collect data but don't use it. They have member information but no insights.

This playbook shows you how to use CRM to build relationships, increase retention, and grow revenue.

---

## Why CRM Matters

### Member Knowledge

**Who are your members?** Demographics, preferences, behavior.

**What do they want?** Goals, interests, needs.

**How do they engage?** Booking patterns, spending, attendance.

### Business Impact

**Retention.** Know who's at risk of leaving.

**Revenue.** Identify upsell opportunities.

**Efficiency.** Automate repetitive tasks.

**Growth.** Find and attract similar members.

---

## Data Collection

### Essential Data

**Contact information.** Name, email, phone, address.

**Demographics.** Age, location, occupation.

**Membership details.** Type, start date, billing.

**Booking history.** What they book, when, how often.

**Payment history.** What they've paid, outstanding balances.

**Communication history.** What they've received, responded to.

### Additional Data

**Skill level.** For coaching and league placement.

**Preferences.** Sport, time, coach, court type.

**Goals.** Why they play, what they want to achieve.

**Referral source.** How they found you.

### Data Quality

**Accuracy.** Keep data current and correct.

**Completeness.** Fill in missing information.

**Consistency.** Use standard formats.

**Privacy.** Comply with data protection regulations.

## Segmentation

### By Activity

**Active.** Booked in the last 30 days.

**Occasional.** Booked in the last 90 days.

**Lapsed.** No booking in 90+ days.

**Inactive.** No booking in 180+ days.

### By Spending

**High-value.** Top 20% of spenders.

**Medium-value.** Middle 60%.

**Low-value.** Bottom 20%.

### By Engagement

**Highly engaged.** Active in leagues, events, coaching.

**Moderately engaged.** Occasional participation.

**Low engagement.** Booking only.

### By Tenure

**New.** Joined in last 90 days.

**Established.** 90 days to 1 year.

**Loyal.** 1+ year.

## Communication Strategy

### Welcome Sequence

**Day 1.** Welcome email with key information.

**Day 3.** Introduction to coaching and programs.

**Day 7.** Invitation to first event or open play.

**Day 14.** Check-in and feedback request.

### Regular Communication

**Newsletter.** Weekly or monthly updates.

**Event promotion.** Upcoming events and programs.

**Personalized offers.** Based on behavior and preferences.

**Birthday messages.** Personal touch.

### Re-engagement

**30 days inactive.** Gentle reminder.

**60 days inactive.** Special offer to return.

**90 days inactive.** Personal outreach.

**180 days inactive.** Final attempt or removal.

## Automated Workflows

### Booking Workflows

**Confirmation.** Immediate booking confirmation.

**Reminder.** 24-hour and 2-hour reminders.

**Follow-up.** Post-booking feedback request.

### Membership Workflows

**Renewal reminder.** Before membership expires.

**Payment failure.** Automatic retry and notification.

**Upgrade suggestion.** Based on usage patterns.

### Coaching Workflows

**Package reminder.** Before sessions expire.

**Progress update.** Monthly coaching progress.

**Re-engagement.** When sessions lapse.

## Analytics and Insights

### Member Metrics

**Retention rate.** Percentage who renew.

**Lifetime value.** Total revenue per member.

**Engagement score.** Activity level.

**Satisfaction score.** Net Promoter Score.

### Business Metrics

**Revenue per member.** Average monthly spend.

**Acquisition cost.** How much it costs to get a member.

**Churn rate.** Percentage who leave.

**Growth rate.** New members minus departures.

### Predictive Analytics

**Churn prediction.** Who's likely to leave.

**Upsell prediction.** Who's likely to buy more.

**LTV prediction.** Future member value.

## FAQ

### What's the most important CRM metric?

Retention rate. It's easier and cheaper to keep existing members than acquire new ones.

### How often should I communicate with members?

Weekly for newsletter. Monthly for personalized offers. As needed for important updates.

### How do I personalize communication?

Use member data to send relevant content based on behavior, preferences, and interests.

### What's the best way to reduce churn?

Proactive engagement, quick issue resolution, and continuous value delivery.
