## Court Essentials

### Padel Balls

**What to stock:**
- Match balls (ITF approved)
- Training balls (durable, lower bounce)
- Junior balls (softer, slower)

**What to sell:**
- Match balls: 3-pack, 6-pack
- Training balls: 6-pack, 12-pack
- Ball cans (branded with your club logo)

**Pricing:**
- Match balls: $15-25 per 3-pack
- Training balls: $12-20 per 6-pack
- Branded cans: $5-10 premium

## Padel Rackets

### Understanding Racket Categories

**Beginner rackets:**
- Light weight (340-360g)
- Round head shape
- Soft core
- Price: $50-100

**Intermediate rackets:**
- Medium weight (360-375g)
- Teardrop shape
- Medium core
- Price: $100-200

**Advanced rackets:**
- Heavy weight (375-390g)
- Diamond shape
- Hard core
- Price: $200-400

### What to Stock

**Rental fleet:**
- 10-20 beginner rackets
- 5-10 intermediate rackets
- 2-3 advanced rackets

**Retail inventory:**
- 5-10 beginner models (bestsellers)
- 3-5 intermediate models
- 2-3 premium models

### Bestseller Models

Stock these for guaranteed sales:
- Bullpadel Hack
- Nox MJ10 Luxury
- Adidas Adipower
- Headpadel
- Babolat

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Too Much Inventory

Stocking too many SKUs ties up cash. Start with essentials and expand based on demand.

### Mistake 2: Wrong Pricing

Price too high and players shop online. Price too low and margins suffer. Match market rates with a small premium for convenience.

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Quality

Cheap equipment breaks fast and frustrates players. Stock quality brands.

### Mistake 4: No Display

Equipment hidden in back rooms doesn't sell. Display prominently.

### Mistake 5: Not Training Staff

Staff should know equipment. They should be able to recommend rackets, explain differences, and upsell.

## FAQ

### What padel equipment should every club stock?

Start with balls, grips, rental rackets, and club-branded apparel. Expand based on player demand.

### How many rental rackets should I have?

10-20 beginner rackets, 5-10 intermediate, 2-3 advanced. Adjust based on club size.

### What are the best-selling padel items?

Balls, grips, overgrips, and club-branded apparel. These have high margins and repeat purchases.

### Should I sell rackets or just rent them?

Both. Sell for ownership, rent for trial. Many players want to try before they buy.
