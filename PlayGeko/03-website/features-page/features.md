## Core Features

### 1. Court Booking & Scheduling

Real-time availability. Instant confirmations. Zero double-bookings.

- **Multi-sport support:** Padel, tennis, squash, basketball, handball, volleyball, badminton, futsal — every court type, one system
- **Real-time calendar:** Players see live availability. No manual updates. No phone calls.
- **Automated reminders:** Email and push notifications before bookings reduce no-shows by up to 40%
- **Flexible scheduling:** Set hours by day, court, and season. Block maintenance windows. Open holiday slots.
- **Recurring bookings:** Members can book weekly slots that auto-renew
- **Waitlist management:** When courts are full, players join a waitlist and get notified when a slot opens

**How it works:** A player opens the app, picks a sport, selects a time, and pays. Done. The court is booked. The coach is notified. The payment is processed. One action, five systems updated.

---

### 2. Payments & POS

Accept payments anywhere — online, at the desk, or through the app.

- **Online checkout:** Players pay when they book. No cash handling. No chasing payments.
- **On-site POS:** Sell day passes, gear, drinks, and coaching sessions at the front desk
- **Membership billing:** Auto-renew monthly and annual memberships with failed payment retry
- **Multi-currency support:** Operate in USD, EUR, or local currency across branches
- **Payout tracking:** See exactly what's owed, what's been collected, and what's pending — per branch, per day
- **Commission control:** 0% on direct bookings. 6% on marketplace-originated bookings. You set the terms.

**Club reality:** Most clubs lose 15-20% of revenue to no-shows and uncollected payments. PlayGeko's prepayment requirement and automated deposit locks eliminate both.

---

### 3. CRM & Member Management

Know every player. Act on what matters.

- **Member profiles:** Full history — bookings, payments, attendance, preferences, lifetime value
- **Custom fields:** Track anything — skill level, medical notes, preferred court, emergency contact
- **Segmentation:** Group members by activity, spending, or engagement. Target the right ones with the right offer.
- **Automated follow-ups:** Trigger emails when a member hasn't booked in 30 days. Send promos to inactive members.
- **Lead tracking:** Capture trial users, convert them to members, track the journey

**Data you actually need:** Not just who booked — but who's at risk of leaving, who's ready for an upgrade, and who hasn't shown up in two weeks.

---

### 4. Coaching & Session Management

Let coaches sell. Let players book. Let the system handle the rest.

- **Coach profiles:** Each coach sets availability, pricing, and session types
- **Session packages:** Sell 5-pack, 10-pack, or unlimited lesson bundles
- **Progress tracking:** Coaches log player improvement, note areas to work on
- **Automated scheduling:** Players book coaching sessions that fit both their calendar and the coach's availability
- **Package expiry alerts:** Players get notified before unused sessions expire

**Revenue driver:** Coaching packages are the highest-margin product for most clubs. PlayGeko makes them easy to sell and track.

---

### 5. Leagues & Match Play

Keep courts full. Keep players coming back.

- **League creation:** Set up weekly, monthly, or seasonal leagues with custom rules
- **Ranking system:** Dynamic ratings that update after every match based on opponent strength and score
- **Match scheduling:** System generates fixtures, assigns courts, and notifies players
- **Score tracking:** Players enter scores. Coaches validate. Rankings update automatically.
- **Open matches:** Any player can create a match. Others join from the app. Courts fill without marketing.

**Why it matters:** Players who join leagues stay 3x longer than casual bookers. Leagues create community. Community creates retention.

---

### 6. Tournament Management

Run professional tournaments without the spreadsheet.

- **Bracket generation:** Single elimination, double elimination, round robin — auto-generated
- **Court assignment:** System assigns courts based on availability and tournament schedule
- **Live results:** Players and spectators see real-time scores and bracket updates
- **Registration & payments:** Collect entry fees through the app. Handle refunds by rule.
- **Multi-branch tournaments:** Run cross-location competitions with centralized brackets

**From 12 courts to 1,200:** The same system that manages your Saturday morning padel league runs a 64-player tournament across three branches.

---

### 7. White-Label Mobile App

Your brand. Your app. Your players.

- **Custom branding:** Your logo, your colors, your name — in the App Store and Google Play
- **Player experience:** Players book, pay, join matches, and track progress — all under your brand
- **Push notifications:** Send promos, match reminders, and league updates directly to players
- **No PlayGeko branding:** The app looks and feels 100% yours
- **Available on iOS and Android**

**Why it matters:** A branded app builds loyalty. Players who use your app book 2.5x more often than those who don't.

---

### 8. Analytics & Reporting

Make decisions based on data. Not gut feeling.

- **Revenue dashboard:** See daily, weekly, and monthly revenue by branch, court, and service
- **Occupancy rates:** Know which courts are underperforming and when
- **Member engagement:** Track booking frequency, spending patterns, and churn risk
- **Coaching metrics:** See which coaches are selling the most, which packages are popular
- **Export reports:** Download data as CSV for accounting or further analysis

**What you see at a glance:** Total reservations this week. Revenue vs. last month. Members who haven't booked in 14 days. Courts with lowest utilization.

---

### 9. Multi-Branch Management

One dashboard. Every location.

- **Centralized control:** Manage all branches from a single admin panel
- **Cross-branch bookings:** Players can book at any location with one account
- **Branch-level reporting:** Compare performance across locations
- **Staff permissions:** Set roles and access levels per branch
- **Unified CRM:** One member profile works across all your locations

**For operators running 2+ branches:** Stop managing separate systems. One login, all branches, real-time data everywhere.

---

### 10. AI-Driven Growth

The platform that learns your business and helps you grow it.

- **Smart recommendations:** AI analyzes booking patterns and suggests pricing adjustments
- **Upsell prompts:** Automatically suggest coaching packages or memberships based on player behavior
- **Churn prediction:** Identify members at risk of leaving before they do
- **Demand forecasting:** Know which hours will be busy. Staff accordingly. Avoid over/under-staffing.
- **Content suggestions:** AI helps you write marketing copy for campaigns and promotions

**What this means:** PlayGeko doesn't just manage your club — it helps you grow it. Smart suggestions based on your actual data, not generic advice.
