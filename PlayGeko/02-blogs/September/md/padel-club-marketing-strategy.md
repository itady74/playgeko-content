## Introduction

Most padel clubs don't have a marketing problem. They have a distribution problem. Players don't know you exist. Or they know but haven't visited. Or they visited but didn't come back.

Marketing isn't about flashy ads. It's about getting the right message to the right players at the right time.

This guide shows you how to build a marketing strategy that fills courts and grows your club.

---

## The Marketing Framework

### Three Channels

**Digital.** Social media, Google, email, marketplace.

**Community.** Events, leagues, partnerships, word-of-mouth.

**Retention.** CRM, loyalty programs, member experience.

Most clubs over-invest in acquisition and under-invest in retention. The most profitable clubs balance all three.

---

## Channel 1: Digital Marketing

### Social Media

**Instagram.** Visual platform. Show courts, players, action shots, behind-the-scenes. Post 3-5 times per week.

**Facebook.** Community groups, event promotion, local targeting. Good for reaching 30+ demographic.

**TikTok.** Short-form video. Show exciting rallies, trick shots, social moments. Reach younger players.

**LinkedIn.** Corporate targeting. B2B marketing for corporate events and team building.

### Content Strategy

**Court photos.** High-quality images of your courts. Morning light, evening atmosphere, action shots.

**Player stories.** Feature your members. Their progress, their experience, their community.

**Behind-the-scenes.** Coaching sessions, tournament preparation, club events.

**Educational content.** Tips, techniques, rules explanations. Position your club as an expert.

**User-generated content.** Repost player content. Tag them. Build community.

### Google Marketing

**Google Business Profile.** Claim and optimize. Photos, hours, reviews, booking link.

**SEO.** Blog content targeting local searches. "Padel court near me," "padel club [city]."

**Google Ads.** Target high-intent searches. "Book padel court [city]."

**Reviews.** Encourage Google reviews. Respond to all reviews.

### Email Marketing

**Welcome sequence.** New member onboarding emails.

**Booking reminders.** Automated before every booking.

**Promotional emails.** Off-peak discounts, new programs, events.

**Re-engagement.** Inactive member win-back campaigns.

**Newsletter.** Monthly club updates, highlights, upcoming events.

## Channel 2: Community Marketing

### Events

**Open days.** Free play sessions for non-members. Let them experience your club.

**Tournaments.** Entry fees, sponsorships, community building.

**Social events.** Mixers, holiday parties, member appreciation nights.

**Clinics.** Guest coaches, specialty workshops, intensive training.

### Partnerships

**Local businesses.** Cross-promote with restaurants, cafes, gyms, hotels.

**Schools and universities.** Youth programs, student discounts, campus outreach.

**Corporate partnerships.** Team building, corporate memberships, wellness programs.

**Sports brands.** Equipment demos, sponsored events, co-marketing.

### Word-of-Mouth

**Referral program.** Give existing members incentives to bring friends.

**Member ambassadors.** Identify your most passionate members. Empower them to spread the word.

**Social proof.** Share testimonials, success stories, and community highlights.

---

## Channel 3: Retention Marketing

### CRM Strategy

**Know your members.** Track booking frequency, spending, and engagement.

**Segment by behavior.** Active, regular, occasional, inactive.

**Automated triggers.** Re-engage inactive members, suggest upgrades, celebrate milestones.

**Personalized communication.** Targeted messages based on individual behavior.

### Loyalty Programs

**Booking credits.** Earn credits for frequent bookings. Redeem for free sessions.

**Coaching discounts.** Members get reduced rates on coaching.

**Priority booking.** Members book before non-members.

**Exclusive events.** Member-only tournaments, socials, and clinics.

### Member Experience

**Smooth booking.** Fast, easy, mobile-first.

**Quality courts.** Well-maintained surfaces, proper lighting, clean facilities.

**Community feel.** Players know each other. Staff know players.

**Responsive support.** Quick answers to questions and issues.

## Budget Allocation

### Recommended Split

**Digital (40%).** Social media, Google, email.

**Community (30%).** Events, partnerships, sponsorships.

**Retention (20%).** CRM, loyalty, member experience.

**Testing (10%).** New channels, experiments, A/B tests.

### Cost Benchmarks

**Social media management:** $500-2,000/month (agency) or staff time.

**Google Ads:** $300-1,000/month.

## Common Marketing Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No Strategy

Posting randomly without a plan wastes time and money.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Retention

Acquiring new members costs 5-7x more than retaining existing ones.

### Mistake 3: Not Tracking Results

If you don't measure, you can't improve.

### Mistake 4: One-Channel Dependency

Relying only on Instagram or only on Google is risky. Diversify.

### Mistake 5: Generic Content

Content that could be for any club doesn't differentiate you. Be specific.

## FAQ

### How much should I spend on padel club marketing?

10-15% of revenue is a good starting point. Adjust based on growth goals and market conditions.

### What's the most effective marketing channel for padel clubs?

It depends on your market. Most clubs see the best ROI from Google Business Profile, Instagram, and community events.

### How do I get more Google reviews?

Ask satisfied members directly. Send a follow-up email with a review link. Make it easy.

### Should I hire a marketing agency?

If you have the budget, yes. Agencies bring expertise and consistency. If not, focus on 2-3 channels and do them well.
