## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does PlayGeko support different court surfaces?

Yes. PlayGeko tracks court surfaces (hard, clay, grass, indoor) separately. Each surface type can have its own scheduling rules, pricing, and maintenance windows.

### Can I run both singles and doubles leagues?

Yes. PlayGeko supports singles, doubles, and mixed doubles leagues in round robin, ladder, and seasonal formats. Rankings update automatically after every match.

### Does PlayGeko integrate with tennis rating systems?

PlayGeko's dynamic leveling system tracks player ratings based on match results and opponent strength. You can also import external ratings (NTRP, UTR) as custom fields.

### Can I manage family memberships?

Yes. PlayGeko supports family accounts where one membership covers multiple players. Each player has their own profile, booking history, and coaching progress.

### How does PlayGeko handle court maintenance?

You can block courts for maintenance by date and time. Players see the court as unavailable. You can set recurring maintenance windows (e.g., clay court rest days).

### Can I sell tennis gear through PlayGeko?

Yes. PlayGeko's POS system handles inventory tracking and sales for rackets, balls, grips, strings, and other tennis equipment.
