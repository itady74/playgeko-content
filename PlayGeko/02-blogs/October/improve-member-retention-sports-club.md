## Introduction

Acquiring a new member costs 5-7x more than retaining an existing one. Yet most clubs focus on acquisition and neglect retention.

Member retention isn't about discounts. It's about building relationships, creating value, and making your club indispensable.

This guide covers proven strategies to keep members engaged and coming back.

---

## Why Members Leave

### Common Reasons

**Lack of engagement.** Members feel like a number, not a person.

**Poor experience.** Bad booking experience, dirty facilities, rude staff.

**No community.** Members don't know other players.

**No progress.** Members aren't improving.

**Better alternatives.** Competitors offer something you don't.

### The Cost

**Lost revenue.** Each departed member costs hundreds in lifetime value.

**Negative word-of-mouth.** Unhappy members tell others.

**Referral loss.** Departed members don't bring friends.

---

## Strategy 1: Know Your Members

### Collect Data

**Demographics.** Age, location, occupation.

**Behavior.** Booking frequency, preferred times, spending patterns.

**Goals.** Why do they play? What do they want to achieve?

**Feedback.** What do they like/dislike?

### Act on Data

**Personalized communication.** Reference their specific activity.

**Targeted offers.** Suggest relevant programs based on behavior.

**Proactive outreach.** Contact members showing signs of disengagement.

---

## Strategy 2: Build Community

### Social Events

**Mixers.** Regular social events for members to meet.

**Tournaments.** Competitive events that build camaraderie.

**Holiday celebrations.** Seasonal events that create tradition.

**Member appreciation.** Events that celebrate your community.

### Group Activities

**Group coaching.** Players learn together.

**Leagues.** Team-based competition.

**Club nights.** Regular social play sessions.

**Interest groups.** Women's groups, senior groups, parent groups.

### Online Community

**Member directory.** Find and connect with other players.

**Group chats.** Communication channels for groups and teams.

**Social features.** Share photos, results, and experiences.

---

## Strategy 3: Create Value

### Beyond Court Access

**Coaching.** Help players improve.

**Community.** Connect players with similar interests.

**Events.** Social and competitive experiences.

**Facilities.** Quality courts and amenities.

**Technology.** Easy booking and communication.

### Exclusive Benefits

**Member-only events.** Special access for members.

**Priority booking.** Members book before non-members.

**Discounts.** Reduced rates on coaching, gear, and events.

**Early access.** First to know about new programs.

---

## Strategy 4: Communicate Effectively

### Communication Cadence

**Welcome.** New member onboarding sequence.

**Regular updates.** Weekly or monthly newsletter.

**Event promotion.** Promote upcoming events and programs.

**Feedback requests.** Regular check-ins and surveys.

**Re-engagement.** Inactive member outreach.

### Channels

**Email.** Newsletters, promotions, updates.

**Push notifications.** Through your branded mobile app.

**SMS.** Critical updates and reminders.

**In-app messaging.** Direct communication.

**Social media.** Community building and content.

---

## Strategy 5: Listen and Act

### Feedback Collection

**Surveys.** Regular satisfaction surveys.

**NPS.** Net Promoter Score tracking.

**Reviews.** Online review monitoring.

**Conversations.** Staff-member interactions.

### Act on Feedback

**Respond quickly.** Acknowledge feedback promptly.

**Make changes.** Implement suggested improvements.

**Communicate changes.** Tell members what you changed based on their feedback.

**Follow up.** Check if changes addressed the issue.

## Strategy 6: Recognition and Rewards

### Recognition

**Milestones.** Celebrate membership anniversaries.

**Achievements.** Recognize player progress.

**Participation.** Acknowledge active members.

**Community contributions.** Thank members who help others.

### Rewards

**Loyalty programs.** Earn points for activity.

**Referral rewards.** Thank members who bring friends.

**Exclusive perks.** Special benefits for loyal members.

**Surprise and delight.** Unexpected gestures that delight.

## Measuring Retention

### Key Metrics

**Retention rate.** Percentage of members who renew.

**Churn rate.** Percentage of members who leave.

**Lifetime value.** Total revenue per member over their tenure.

**Engagement score.** Composite measure of member activity.

### Tools

**CRM analytics.** Track member behavior and trends.

**Cohort analysis.** Compare retention across groups.

**Predictive analytics.** Identify members at risk of leaving.

## FAQ

### What's a good retention rate for sports clubs?

70-80% is good. 80%+ is excellent. Below 60% needs immediate attention.

### How do I identify members at risk of leaving?

Look for declining booking frequency, reduced spending, and lower engagement. PlayGeko flags these automatically.

### What's the most effective retention strategy?

Community building. Members who form relationships with other players are far less likely to leave.

### How often should I survey members?

Quarterly surveys for feedback. NPS monthly. Ad-hoc surveys for specific topics.
