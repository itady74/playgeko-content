## Technology Considerations

### Mobile Experience

**Player-facing app.** Is it your brand or theirs? Can players book, pay, and communicate?

**Staff-facing app.** Can staff manage operations on the go?

**Progressive web app.** Does it work well on mobile browsers?

### Integration Capabilities

**Payment gateways.** Which processors are supported?

**Accounting software.** Does it integrate with QuickBooks, Xero, or similar?

**Marketing tools.** Email marketing, social media, CRM integrations.

**API availability.** Can you build custom integrations?

### Security and Compliance

**Data encryption.** Is data encrypted at rest and in transit?

**GDPR compliance.** Does it handle data privacy requirements?

**PCI compliance.** For payment processing.

**Backup and recovery.** How is data backed up and recovered?

## Pricing Models

### Subscription SaaS

**Monthly fee.** Predictable costs. Scales with usage.

**Per-user pricing.** Cost increases with more staff or members.

**Tiered pricing.** Different features at different price points.

### Transaction-Based

**Commission on bookings.** Percentage of each booking.

**Payment processing fees.** Built-in payment processing.

### One-Time License

**Upfront cost.** Higher initial investment, lower ongoing costs.

**Maintenance fees.** Annual maintenance and support.

### What to Watch For

**Hidden fees.** Setup fees, training fees, support fees.

**Contract length.** Month-to-month vs. annual contracts.

**Scaling costs.** How pricing changes as you grow.

## Red Flags

### Red Flag 1: No Free Trial

If a vendor won't let you try before you buy, they're not confident in their product.

### Red Flag 2: Long Contracts

Month-to-month flexibility is standard. Multi-year contracts lock you in.

### Red Flag 3: Hidden Costs

Setup fees, training fees, support fees — these should be transparent upfront.

### Red Flag 4: Poor Mobile Experience

If the mobile app is clunky or nonexistent, players won't use it.

### Red Flag 5: No Integrations

If the platform doesn't integrate with your existing tools, you'll be stuck doing things manually.

### Red Flag 6: Slow Support

When something breaks, you need help fast. Slow support costs you revenue.

## Decision Framework

### Step 1: Define Requirements

List your must-haves and nice-to-haves. Rank by importance.

### Step 2: Shortlist Vendors

Research 3-5 vendors that meet your core requirements.

### Step 3: Request Demos

See each platform in action. Ask specific questions about your use case.

### Step 4: Try Before You Buy

Sign up for free trials. Test with real data and real workflows.

### Step 5: Check References

Talk to existing customers. Ask about implementation, support, and results.

### Step 6: Negotiate

Don't accept the first price. Negotiate based on commitment and volume.

### Step 7: Plan Implementation

Define timeline, responsibilities, and success metrics.
