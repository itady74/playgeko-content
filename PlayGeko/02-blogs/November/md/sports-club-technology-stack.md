## Introduction

Technology isn't optional for modern sports clubs. It's how you compete, grow, and serve members.

But most clubs have fragmented systems. A booking tool here. A payment system there. No integration.

This guide covers the essential technology stack for sports clubs.

---

## Core Technology

### 1. Club Management System

**The foundation.** Everything connects here.

**Must-have features:**
- Court booking
- Member management
- Payments
- Communication

**Nice-to-have:**
- League management
- Coaching management
- Analytics
- Mobile app

### 2. Payment Processing

**Integrated payments.** Connected to your management system.

**Must-have:**
- Credit/debit cards
- Digital wallets
- Online and on-site payments

**Nice-to-have:**
- Recurring billing
- Multi-currency
- Payment plans

### 3. Website

**Online presence.** Information and booking.

**Must-have:**
- Responsive design
- Online booking
- Member portal
- Contact information

**Nice-to-have:**
- Blog
- Events calendar
- Member directory

### 4. Mobile App

**Player-facing.** Booking and communication on the go.

**Must-have:**
- Court booking
- Push notifications
- Member profile

**Nice-to-have:**
- In-app messaging
- League features
- Social features

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Fragmented Systems

Multiple disconnected tools create data silos and inefficiency.

### Mistake 2: Too Much Technology

Don't over-complicate. Start with essentials and add as needed.

### Mistake 3: No Integration

Systems that don't talk to each other waste time and create errors.

### Mistake 4: Ignoring Mobile

Players expect mobile access. Don't neglect mobile experience.

### Mistake 5: No Analytics

If you don't measure, you can't improve.

## FAQ

### What's the most important technology for sports clubs?

Club management system. It's the foundation everything else connects to.

### How much should I spend on technology?

5-10% of revenue is a good starting point. Adjust based on needs and growth.

### Should I use an all-in-one system or best-of-breed?

All-in-one is simpler. Best-of-breed may be better for specific needs. Consider your capacity to manage integrations.

### How do I choose the right tools?

Define your needs. Research options. Test before you buy. Check references.
