## Court Booking for Padel Clubs

The booking system is the heartbeat of your club. If it's slow, confusing, or unreliable, players go elsewhere.

### What Padel Booking Looks Like in PlayGeko

**Group bookings.** One player reserves a court and shares a booking link with 3 others. Each confirms their spot. Payment is split. Court is booked. Reminder sent.

**Real-time availability.** Players see live court status. No phone calls. No WhatsApp back-and-forth. No double-bookings.

**Recurring bookings.** Members reserve their weekly Thursday evening slot. It auto-renews. They never miss a game.

**Waitlist management.** When courts are full, players join a queue. They get notified when a slot opens. No empty courts when someone cancels.

**Automated reminders.** Push and email notifications before every booking. Clubs using reminders see 40% fewer no-shows.

### The Revenue Impact

A padel club with 6 courts running at 70% occupancy generates roughly $18,000/month from bookings alone. That's with no coaching, no pro shop, no events.

When you add prepayment (eliminating no-shows), recurring bookings (increasing frequency), and marketplace listings (attracting new players), that same club can reach $25,000-30,000/month.

## Leagues & Match Play

Players who join leagues stay 3x longer than casual bookers. Leagues create community. Community creates retention. Retention creates revenue.

### Padel League Formats in PlayGeko

**2v2 leagues.** The standard padel format. Teams of two compete in round robin, ladder, or seasonal formats.

**Open matches.** Any player creates a match. Others join from the app. Courts fill without marketing.

**Dynamic rankings.** Player ratings update after every match based on opponent strength and score. No manual calculations.

**Seasonal leagues.** Run weekly, monthly, or seasonal competitions with automated fixtures and standings.

**Cross-branch leagues.** If you operate multiple locations, run leagues that span all branches.

### Why Leagues Matter for Padel Clubs

Casual players book once a month. League players book every week. The difference in lifetime value is enormous.

A casual player might spend $50/month. A league player spends $150-200/month (booking + coaching + events + pro shop). Over 12 months, that's $600 vs. $2,400.

Leagues also create social bonds. Players who play together form groups. Groups book together. Courts stay full.
