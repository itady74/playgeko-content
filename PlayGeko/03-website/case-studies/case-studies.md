## Featured Case Studies

### Mountain Breeze — Multi-Sport Facility

**Challenge:** Mountain Breeze operated padel, tennis, and basketball courts across multiple locations. They used separate systems for each sport and each branch. Data was fragmented. Staff trained on three different platforms. Member experience was inconsistent.

**Solution:** PlayGeko unified all sports and branches into one platform. Members use a single account across every location. Staff manage all operations from one dashboard.

**Results:**
- Unified member experience across all branches
- Staff training time reduced from weeks to days
- Cross-sport bookings increased 25%
- Revenue visibility improved with centralized reporting

---

### WePadel — Dedicated Padel Operator

**Challenge:** WePadel operated dedicated padel facilities with high player volume. No-shows were costing 15-20% of potential revenue. Manual booking management couldn't keep up with demand.

**Solution:** PlayGeko's prepayment requirement eliminated no-shows. Automated booking reminders reduced cancellations. The marketplace listing attracted new players.

**Results:**
- No-shows reduced by 40%
- New player acquisition increased through marketplace
- Coaching package sales grew 30%
- Tournament management automated end-to-end

---

### Orthodox Club — Padel and Tennis

**Challenge:** Orthodox Club needed a system that handled both padel and tennis. They wanted to offer cross-sport memberships but lacked the technology to manage them.

**Solution:** PlayGeko's multi-sport support enabled cross-sport memberships. Members book padel on Monday, tennis on Wednesday — all from one app.

**Results:**
- Cross-sport memberships increased average member value
- Booking process simplified for members and staff
- League management automated for both sports
- Member retention improved with unified experience

### Oxford Schools — Academic Sports Programs

**Challenge:** Oxford Schools needed to manage sports programs for students across multiple facilities. They needed parent access, scheduling for different age groups, and payment processing for programs.

**Solution:** PlayGeko's flexible platform adapted to academic sports management. Parent accounts, student profiles, and program scheduling all managed from one system.

**Results:**
- Parent access to student booking and payment history
- Program scheduling automated across age groups
- Payment processing streamlined for school programs
- Administrative overhead reduced significantly

### Dunes Club — Premium Multi-Sport

**Results:**

- Premium booking experience matched club brand

- Club identity strengthened with white-label solution

### Vamos Sport Club — Community Sports
