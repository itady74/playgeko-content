## Introduction

No-shows are revenue killers. A 10% no-show rate means 10% of your bookings generate zero revenue. For a club with 100 weekly bookings, that's $500-1,000 in lost revenue per week.

The solution isn't hoping players show up. It's building systems that make showing up easier than not showing up.

This guide covers proven strategies to reduce no-shows and recover lost revenue.

---

## Strategy 1: Prepayment

### Why It Works

Prepayment creates commitment. When players pay upfront, they're invested. The psychological shift from "I might go" to "I've paid, I'm going" is powerful.

### How to Implement

**Require prepayment for all bookings.** No exceptions.

**Offer flexible cancellation.** Full refund up to 24 hours before. Partial refund 12-24 hours before. No refund within 12 hours.

**Accept multiple payment methods.** Credit card, debit card, digital wallets. Make it easy to pay.

### The Numbers

Clubs that require prepayment see 40-60% fewer no-shows. The revenue lost from cancellations is far less than the revenue lost from no-shows.

---

## Strategy 2: Waitlists

### Why It Works

Waitlists fill cancellations instantly. When someone cancels, the next person on the waitlist gets the slot. Empty courts become revenue.

### How to Implement

**Enable waitlists for all courts.** Players can join waitlists for fully booked courts.

**Automatic notification.** When a court opens, notify waitlisted players immediately.

**Time limit.** Give waitlisted players 30 minutes to claim the slot before moving to the next person.

### The Result

Waitlists turn cancellations into bookings. Players appreciate the opportunity. Your courts stay full.

---

## Strategy 3: Automated Reminders

### Why It Works

People forget. Life gets busy. Automated reminders bring your booking back to mind.

### Reminder Schedule

**24 hours before.** "Your booking is tomorrow at 5 PM. See you on court!"

**2 hours before.** "Your court is ready! See you in 2 hours."

**30 minutes before.** "Your booking starts in 30 minutes. Don't forget your gear!"

### Channel

**Push notification.** Through your branded mobile app.

**Email.** Backup for players without app notifications enabled.

**SMS.** For critical reminders (optional, higher cost).

### The Numbers

Automated reminders reduce no-shows by 20-30%. They're the lowest-effort, highest-impact strategy.

---

## Strategy 4: Cancellation Policies

### Why It Works

Clear cancellation policies set expectations. Players know the rules upfront.

### Policy Structure

**24+ hours notice.** Full refund or credit.

**12-24 hours notice.** 50% refund or credit.

**Under 12 hours.** No refund.

**No-show.** No refund, booking fee forfeited.

### Enforcement

**Automated enforcement.** Let the system handle refunds and charges.

**Consistent application.** Apply to everyone equally.

**Clear communication.** Display policy during booking.

---

## Strategy 5: Social Accountability

### Why It Works

When players book with others, they feel responsible to show up. Letting down a friend is harder than letting down a faceless system.

### How to Implement

**Group bookings.** Require group bookings to designate a primary contact.

**Doubles partnerships.** When one player cancels, their partner is notified.

**League play.** Teams are accountable to each other.

**Community features.** Players see who else is playing.

---

## Strategy 6: Loyalty and Consequences

### Positive Reinforcement

**Booking credits.** Earn credits for showing up consistently.

**Priority booking.** Reliable bookers get early access to prime slots.

**Free sessions.** After X bookings, earn a free session.

### Negative Consequences

**No-show tracking.** Track no-shows per player.

**Booking limits.** Players with excessive no-shows lose booking privileges.

**Deposit requirements.** Frequent no-shows require deposits.

---

## Strategy 7: Communication

### Pre-Booking

**Clear expectations.** Tell players about cancellation policies before they book.

**Confirmation emails.** Confirm booking details immediately.

### Post-Booking

**Engagement.** Send helpful information (what to bring, weather, court location).

**Community.** Build relationships so players feel connected to your club.

### Post-No-Show

**Follow-up.** Send a polite message after a no-show. Ask if everything is okay.

**Education.** Remind players of the cancellation policy.

## FAQ

### What's the average no-show rate for sports clubs?

10-20% without intervention. 5-10% with basic measures. Under 5% with comprehensive strategy.

### Is prepayment too aggressive?

No. Players expect prepayment for most bookings. Flexible cancellation policies make it fair.

Clear communication upfront prevents complaints. Most players appreciate fair, consistent policies.
