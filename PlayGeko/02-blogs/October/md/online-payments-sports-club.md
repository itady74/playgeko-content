**Speed.** No waiting in line. No searching for cash.

### For Clubs

**Faster collection.** Money arrives immediately.

**Reduced admin.** No manual processing. No chasing invoices.

**Better cash flow.** Predictable revenue.

**Data.** Payment data informs business decisions.

## Payment Options

### Credit and Debit Cards

**The standard.** Everyone has one. Low friction.

**Fees:** 2.5-3.5% per transaction.

**Best for:** General payments, memberships, day passes.

### Digital Wallets

**Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay.** Tap and go.

**Fees:** Same as card processing.

**Best for:** Mobile bookings, on-site payments.

### Bank Transfers

**Lower fees.** 1-2% per transaction.

**Slower settlement.** 1-3 business days.

**Best for:** Large transactions, memberships, recurring payments.

### Payment Plans

**Split large payments.** Coaching packages, annual memberships.

**Automation.** Automatic installment collection.

**Best for:** High-value purchases, memberships.

## Setting Up Payment Processing

### Step 1: Choose a Payment Gateway

**Stripe.** Most popular. Easy integration. Good documentation.

**PayPal.** Widely trusted. Easy setup. Higher fees.

**Square.** Good for in-person payments. POS integration.

**Adyen.** Enterprise-grade. Multi-currency support.

### Step 2: Integrate with Your Platform

**API integration.** Direct integration with your club management system.

**Third-party integration.** Through your platform's payment module.

**Manual integration.** Standalone payment links.

### Step 3: Configure Payment Options

**Accepted methods.** Cards, digital wallets, bank transfers.

**Currencies.** Local currency and multi-currency if needed.

**Recurring payments.** For memberships and subscriptions.

**Refund policies.** How refunds are handled.

### Step 4: Test Thoroughly

**Test transactions.** Process test payments before going live.

**Test refunds.** Verify refund process works correctly.

**Test edge cases.** Failed payments, expired cards, insufficient funds.

**Go live.** Switch to production mode.

## Payment Flows

### Online Booking Flow

1. Player selects court and time
2. Reviews booking details
3. Enters payment information
4. Confirms payment
5. Receives confirmation

### Membership Billing Flow

1. Member signs up for membership
2. Enters payment information
3. System charges automatically each billing cycle
4. Member receives receipt
5. System handles failed payments

### Coaching Package Flow

1. Player selects coaching package
2. Reviews package details
3. Pays for package upfront
4. Sessions tracked as used

## Security Considerations

### PCI Compliance

**Required.** If you handle card data, you must be PCI compliant.

**Safer option.** Use a payment gateway that handles card data. Don't store card information yourself.

### Fraud Prevention

**Address verification.** Match billing address.

**CVV verification.** Require card verification code.

**3D Secure.** Additional authentication for online payments.

### Data Protection

**Encryption.** All payment data encrypted in transit and at rest.

**Access controls.** Limit who can access payment data.

**Audit trails.** Log all payment activities.

## Pricing and Fees

### Transaction Fees

**Credit/debit cards:** 2.5-3.5% per transaction.

**Digital wallets:** Same as card processing.

**Bank transfers:** 1-2% per transaction.

**ACH transfers:** $0.20-0.50 per transaction.

### Monthly Fees

**Some processors:** Monthly fee + lower transaction rates.

**Others:** No monthly fee but higher transaction rates.

### Choosing the Right Model

**High volume:** Monthly fee + lower rates saves money.

**Low volume:** No monthly fee with higher rates is better.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Cash-Only

Cash is inconvenient for players and risky for clubs. Embrace digital payments.

### Mistake 2: Too Many Steps

Long, complicated checkout processes cause abandonment. Keep it simple.

## FAQ

### What's the best payment processor for sports clubs?

Stripe is the most popular for online payments. Square is good for on-site POS. Your platform may have a preferred processor.

### How long does setup take?

Basic setup: 1-2 hours. Full integration with your club management system: 1-2 weeks.

### What fees should I expect?

2.5-3.5% per card transaction. Some processors have monthly fees with lower rates.

### Can I accept cash and digital payments?

Yes. Many clubs accept both. Track all transactions digitally regardless of payment method.
