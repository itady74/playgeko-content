## Introduction

Digital marketing isn't optional for sports clubs. It's how players find you, learn about you, and decide to join.

But most clubs do digital marketing poorly. Random social posts. No strategy. No measurement.

This guide shows you how to do digital marketing right.

---

## The Digital Marketing Framework

### Four Channels

**Search.** Google, SEO, paid ads.

**Social.** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn.

**Email.** Newsletters, automated sequences.

**Content.** Blog, video, podcast.

### The Goal

Get discovered. Build trust. Convert to members. Retain and grow.

---

## Channel 1: Search Marketing

### SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

**Local SEO.** Optimize for "padel court near me," "tennis club [city]."

**Google Business Profile.** Claim and optimize. Photos, hours, reviews.

**Website SEO.** Optimize pages for relevant keywords.

**Content SEO.** Blog content targeting search queries.

### Google Ads

**Search ads.** Target high-intent keywords.

**Display ads.** Build awareness with visual ads.

**Remarketing.** Target people who visited your website.

**Budget:** Start with $500-1,000/month. Adjust based on results.

### Google Business Profile Optimization

**Complete profile.** All information filled out.

**Photos.** High-quality images of your facility.

**Reviews.** Encourage and respond to reviews.

**Posts.** Regular updates and promotions.

**Q&A.** Answer common questions.

---

## Channel 2: Social Media

### Platform Selection

**Instagram.** Visual content. Courts, players, action.

**Facebook.** Community, events, local targeting.

**TikTok.** Short-form video. Reach younger players.

**LinkedIn.** Corporate targeting. B2B marketing.

### Content Strategy

**Court photos.** High-quality images of your facility.

**Player stories.** Feature your members.

**Behind-the-scenes.** Coaching sessions, tournaments, events.

**Educational content.** Tips, techniques, rules.

**User-generated content.** Repost player content.

### Posting Schedule

**Instagram:** 3-5 posts per week, daily stories.

**Facebook:** 2-3 posts per week.

**TikTok:** 3-5 videos per week.

**LinkedIn:** 1-2 posts per week.

### Engagement

**Respond to comments.** Quickly and thoughtfully.

**Direct messages.** Answer questions promptly.

**Community building.** Foster conversation.

**Collaborations.** Partner with influencers and businesses.

---

## Channel 3: Email Marketing

### Email Types

**Welcome sequence.** Onboard new members.

**Newsletter.** Regular updates and content.

**Promotional.** Events, offers, programs.

**Re-engagement.** Win back inactive members.

**Transactional.** Booking confirmations, receipts.

### Email Best Practices

**Subject lines.** Clear, compelling, specific.

**Content.** Valuable, relevant, concise.

**CTA.** One clear action per email.

**Timing.** Test and optimize send times.

**Segmentation.** Target based on behavior and preferences.

### Automation

**Welcome sequence.** 3-5 emails over 2 weeks.

**Booking reminders.** Before every booking.

**Re-engagement.** After 30 days of inactivity.

**Birthday.** Automated birthday messages.

**Package expiry.** Before coaching packages expire.

## Channel 4: Content Marketing

### Blog

**SEO content.** Target search queries.

## Measurement

### Key Metrics

**Website traffic.** Visitors to your site.

**Conversion rate.** Visitors who become members.

**Cost per acquisition.** How much it costs to acquire a member.

**Engagement rate.** Social media interactions.

**Email open rate.** Percentage who open emails.

**Retention rate.** Percentage who renew.

### Tools

**Google Analytics.** Website traffic and conversions.

**Social media analytics.** Platform-specific metrics.

**Email analytics.** Open rates, click rates.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No Strategy

Random posting without a plan wastes time and money.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Local SEO

Most players search locally. Optimize for local search.

### Mistake 3: One-Channel Dependency

Diversify across multiple channels.

### Mistake 4: Not Measuring

If you don't measure, you can't improve.

### Mistake 5: Generic Content

Content that could be for any club doesn't differentiate you.

## FAQ

### How much should I spend on digital marketing?

10-15% of revenue is a good starting point. Adjust based on growth goals.

### What's the most effective channel?

It depends on your market. Most clubs see best ROI from Google Business Profile and Instagram.

### How do I create content?

Start with what you know. Court photos, player stories, tips. Keep it simple and authentic.

### How do I measure ROI?

Track cost per acquisition. Compare marketing spend to new member revenue.
