## Leagues & Match Play

Leagues create community. Community creates retention. Retention creates revenue.

### Tennis League Formats in PlayGeko

**Singles leagues.** Individual competition in round robin, ladder, or seasonal formats.

**Doubles leagues.** Team-based competition with partner matching.

**Mixed doubles.** Combined gender leagues with appropriate formatting.

**Cross-club competitions.** Run leagues that span multiple locations.

**Open matches.** Any player creates a match. Others join from the app. Courts fill without marketing.

### Why Leagues Matter

Casual players book once a month. League players book every week. The difference in lifetime value is enormous.

A casual player might spend $50/month. A league player spends $150-200/month (booking + coaching + events + pro shop). Over 12 months, that's $600 vs. $2,400.
