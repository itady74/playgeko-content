## Core Features for Handball Clubs

### Court Booking

- **Indoor court scheduling:** Handball courts with proper dimensions and surface requirements
- **Team practice slots:** Teams reserve training times
- **Match scheduling:** System generates fixtures based on league rules and court availability
- **Event blocking:** Reserve courts for tournaments, camps, and special events
- **Automated reminders:** Teams receive notifications before matches and practices

### League Management

- **Team registration:** Full roster management with player eligibility tracking
- **Schedule generation:** Automated fixtures based on court availability and team preferences
- **Standings tracking:** Win/loss records, goal difference, head-to-head tiebreakers
- **Playoff brackets:** Auto-generated from regular season standings
- **Yellow/red card tracking:** Monitor player discipline across the season

### Tournament Management

- **Bracket generation:** Single elimination, round robin, group stage + knockout
- **Referee assignment:** Schedule officials for tournament matches
- **Court assignment:** System assigns courts based on match importance and availability
- **Live results:** Real-time scores and bracket updates
- **Registration & payments:** Collect entry fees through the app

### Team Management

- **Roster management:** Add/remove players, track eligibility, manage substitutions
- **Coach profiles:** Each coach manages their team's schedule and communications
- **Player profiles:** Track individual stats, attendance, and development
- **Team communications:** Send announcements to specific teams
