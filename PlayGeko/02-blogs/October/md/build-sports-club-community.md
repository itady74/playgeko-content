## Introduction

Courts fill calendars. Community fills hearts.

Players can book courts anywhere. They stay where they feel connected. Community is your competitive advantage.

This guide shows you how to build a sports club community that keeps players coming back.

---

## Why Community Matters

### Retention

Players who form relationships with other players are 3x more likely to renew.

### Word-of-Month

Connected players bring friends. Community is your best marketing.

### Revenue

Community members spend more. They book more, buy more, and try more services.

### Experience

Community transforms a facility into a destination. Players come for the courts, stay for the people.

## Strategy 1: Social Events

### Regular Events

**Club nights.** Weekly social play sessions. No booking required. Just show up.

**Mixers.** Social events for players to meet. Random partner draws. Ice-breaker activities.

**Holiday celebrations.** Seasonal events that create tradition.

**Member appreciation.** Events that celebrate your community.

### Special Events

**Tournaments.** Competitive events that build camaraderie.

**Clinics.** Guest coaches, specialty workshops.

**Social parties.** End-of-season celebrations, holiday parties.

**Family days.** Events for members and their families.

### Event Checklist

- [ ] Define purpose and audience
- [ ] Set date and time
- [ ] Book venue/courts
- [ ] Create promotion materials
- [ ] Send invitations
- [ ] Plan activities
- [ ] Arrange food and drinks
- [ ] Set up registration
- [ ] Plan follow-up

## Strategy 2: Group Activities

### Group Coaching

**Beginner groups.** Players learn together.

**Skill clinics.** Focused training sessions.

**Fitness groups.** Conditioning and training.

### Leagues

**Round robin.** Everyone plays everyone.

**Doubles leagues.** Partner-based competition.

**Team leagues.** Group competition.

**Social leagues.** Fun-focused, less competitive.

### Club Nights

**Open play.** Show up and play.

**Themed nights.** Women's night, men's night, beginners' night.

**Skill-based grouping.** Players matched by ability.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Events Without Follow-Up

Events are great. But without follow-up, connections fade. Introduce people. Follow up after events.

### Mistake 2: No Entry Points

Players don't know how to get involved. Create clear entry points for new members.

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Online Community

Online community extends physical community. Don't neglect it.

### Mistake 4: One-Way Communication

Communication isn't just announcements. It's listening and responding.

### Mistake 5: Not Measuring

If you don't measure community health, you can't improve it.

### How do I get players to attend events?

Track event attendance, group participation, member connections, and retention rates.
