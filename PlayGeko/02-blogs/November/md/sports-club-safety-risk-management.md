## Introduction

Safety isn't a checkbox. It's a culture. One accident can destroy your club's reputation and finances.

Most clubs under-invest in safety until something goes wrong. Don't be most clubs.

This guide covers essential safety and risk management for sports clubs.

---

## Facility Safety

### Court Safety

**Surface maintenance.** Regular inspection and repair.

**Lighting.** Proper illumination for safe play.

**Equipment.** Nets, posts, and accessories in good condition.

**Signage.** Clear safety rules and warnings.

### Building Safety

**Fire safety.** Extinguishers, alarms, evacuation plans.

**Electrical safety.** Regular inspections, proper wiring.

**HVAC.** Proper ventilation and temperature control.

**Accessibility.** ADA compliance for all members.

### Outdoor Safety

**Weather protection.** Shade, wind protection.

**Drainage.** Proper water management.

**Fencing.** Secure perimeter.

**Lighting.** Adequate outdoor lighting.

## Emergency Procedures

### Emergency Plan

**Written plan.** Clear, documented procedures.

**Roles.** Who does what in an emergency.

**Communication.** How to alert staff and members.

**Evacuation.** Safe evacuation routes and assembly points.

### First Aid

**First aid kits.** Accessible and fully stocked.

**Trained staff.** At least one staff member trained in first aid/CPR.

**AED.** Automated external defibrillator on-site.

**Emergency contacts.** Posted and easily accessible.

### Incident Response

**Immediate response.** First aid, call emergency services.

**Documentation.** Record what happened.

**Communication.** Notify management and insurance.

**Follow-up.** Ensure proper treatment and prevention.

**Activity waivers.** For specific activities.

**Photo releases.** For marketing use.

**Minor agreements.** Parental consent for youth members.

### Policies

**Safety rules.** Clear, posted, enforced.

**Code of conduct.** Expected behavior.

**Incident reporting.** How to report issues.

**Complaint resolution.** How to address concerns.

## Risk Assessment

### Identify Risks

**Physical risks.** Facility, equipment, activities.

**Operational risks.** Staffing, scheduling, technology.

**Financial risks.** Revenue, expenses, cash flow.

**Reputational risks.** Reviews, social media, word-of-mouth.

### Assess Risks

**Likelihood.** How likely is it to occur?

**Impact.** How bad would it be?

**Priority.** Focus on high-likelihood, high-impact risks.

### Mitigate Risks

**Prevention.** Stop risks before they occur.

**Transfer.** Insurance, contracts, waivers.

**Accept.** Some risks are acceptable with monitoring.

**Avoid.** Eliminate unacceptable risks.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: No Written Plan

If it's not written down, it doesn't exist. Document everything.

### Mistake 2: No Training

Plans are useless if staff don't know them. Train regularly.

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Small Issues

Small issues become big problems. Address them early.

### Mistake 4: No Insurance

One accident without insurance can destroy your club.

### Mistake 5: Complacency

Safety requires constant vigilance. Don't get comfortable.

## FAQ

### What's the most important safety measure?

A written emergency plan with trained staff. Everything else builds on that.

### Do I need an AED?

Yes. Sudden cardiac arrest is a leading cause of death. An AED can save lives.

### How often should I conduct safety training?

Quarterly for all staff. Monthly for key personnel. Annual comprehensive training.

### What insurance do I need?

General liability, property, professional liability, and workers' compensation at minimum.
