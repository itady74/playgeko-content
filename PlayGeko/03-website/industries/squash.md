- **Box leagues:** The classic squash format — players grouped in boxes of 4-6, promoted/relegated monthly
- **Ladder leagues:** Players challenge opponents above them
- **Team leagues:** Club vs. club competitions with roster management
- **Dynamic rankings:** Player ratings update after every match
- **Match scheduling:** System generates fixtures and notifies players

- **Referee assignment:** Assign officials to matches automatically
