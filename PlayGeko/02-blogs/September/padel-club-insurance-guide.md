## Introduction

Insurance isn't exciting. But it's essential. One injury lawsuit can destroy a club that took years to build.

If you're running a padel club, you need proper insurance coverage. This guide covers what you need, what to watch out for, and how to protect your business.

---

## Essential Insurance Types

### 1. General Liability Insurance

**What it covers:** Third-party injuries and property damage.

**Examples:**
- Player slips and breaks ankle on your court
- Guest trips over equipment and gets injured
- Ball hits spectator and causes injury

**Coverage needed:** $1-2 million per occurrence, $2-5 million aggregate.

**Cost:** $1,000-3,000/year for small clubs.

---

### 2. Property Insurance

**What it covers:** Your physical assets — building, courts, equipment.

**Examples:**
- Fire damages courts
- Vandalism destroys equipment
- Storm damages building

**Coverage needed:** Replacement value of all assets.

**Cost:** Varies by property value and location.

---

### 3. Professional Liability Insurance

**What it covers:** Claims of professional negligence.

**Examples:**
- Coach gives bad advice that causes injury
- Player claims coaching caused harm
- Training program causes injury

**Coverage needed:** $1-2 million per occurrence.

**Cost:** $500-1,500/year per coach.

---

### 4. Workers' Compensation

**What it covers:** Employee injuries on the job.

**Examples:**
- Staff member injured during court maintenance
- Coach injured during demonstration
- Front desk staff injured by falling equipment

**Required:** Yes, in most jurisdictions.

**Cost:** Varies by payroll and risk level.

---

### 5. Tournament Insurance

**What it covers:** Tournament-specific risks.

**Examples:**
- Player injury during tournament
- Equipment damage during tournament
- Spectator injury at tournament

**Coverage needed:** Per tournament, typically $1-2 million.

**Cost:** $200-500 per tournament.

---

### 6. Cyber Liability Insurance

**What it covers:** Data breaches and cyber attacks.

**Examples:**
- Member data is stolen
- Payment information is compromised
- Website is hacked

**Coverage needed:** $500,000-1 million.

**Cost:** $500-1,500/year.

---

## What to Watch Out For

### Exclusions

**Read the fine print.** Most policies have exclusions. Common ones:

- **Professional coaching:** Many general liability policies exclude coaching. You need separate professional liability.
- **Tournaments:** Often excluded. Need tournament-specific coverage.
- **Equipment:** May not cover specialized padel equipment.
- **Outdoor courts:** May have different terms than indoor courts.

### Coverage Limits

**Per occurrence vs. aggregate.** Per occurrence is max per incident. Aggregate is max per year. Make sure both are adequate.

**Sub-limits.** Some policies have lower limits for specific items. Check equipment sub-limits.

### Deductibles

**Higher deductible = lower premium.** But make sure you can afford the deductible if you need to file a claim.

- [ ] Report claims immediately

## Cost Management

### Ways to Reduce Premiums

**Safety measures.** Well-maintained courts, proper lighting, clear signage.

**Staff training.** Trained staff have fewer accidents.

**Claims history.** Clean history means lower premiums.

**Bundle policies.** Multiple policies with one insurer often get discounts.

**Higher deductible.** Higher deductible = lower premium.

### Cost vs. Risk

Don't cut corners on insurance. The cost of a claim far exceeds the cost of proper coverage.

## FAQ

### How much does padel club insurance cost?

Varies by club size and coverage. Small clubs: $2,000-5,000/year. Larger clubs: $5,000-15,000/year.

### Do I need insurance for tournaments?

Yes. Tournament-specific insurance is essential. Many venues require proof of coverage.

### Does general liability cover coaching?

Usually not. You need separate professional liability insurance for coaching.

### What's the minimum coverage I need?

Minimum $1-2 million general liability. Higher for larger clubs or those with coaching programs.
