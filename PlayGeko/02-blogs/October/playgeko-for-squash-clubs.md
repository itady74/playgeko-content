## Box Leagues

### What Are Box Leagues?

Groups of 4-8 players compete against each other. Top players move up. Bottom players move down.

### How PlayGeko Manages Box Leagues

**Automatic grouping.** System creates boxes based on rankings.

**Schedule management.** Players schedule matches within their box.

**Result tracking.** Players enter results. Rankings update automatically.

**Promotion/relegation.** Top players move up. Bottom players move down.

### Benefits

**Regular competition.** Players have ongoing matches.

**Clear progression.** Players can see their improvement.

**Community building.** Players compete against similar-level opponents.
