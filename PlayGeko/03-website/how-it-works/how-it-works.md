## The Setup Process

### Step 1: Add Your Club (15 minutes)

Tell us about your club. We'll set up the rest.

- **Club name and location:** Basic info that appears in your branded app
- **Sports and court types:** Padel, tennis, squash, basketball — whatever you operate
- **Court count and layout:** How many courts, surface types, and dimensions
- **Operating hours:** Standard hours, seasonal adjustments, maintenance windows
- **Staff accounts:** Add coaches, front desk staff, and administrators with role-based access

**What happens here:** PlayGeko creates your club profile, generates your branded app preview, and provisions your admin dashboard. Everything is pre-configured for your sport mix.

### Step 2: Configure Bookings & Payments (30 minutes)

Set the rules. Start taking money.

- **Booking rules:** Advance booking window, cancellation policy, maximum group size
- **Pricing:** Set rates by court type, time slot, and day of week. Create peak/off-peak pricing.
- **Payment setup:** Connect your Stripe account. Settle on online and on-site payment options.
- **Membership tiers:** Define monthly and annual membership levels with pricing and perks
- **Coaching packages:** Set up 5-pack, 10-pack, and custom lesson bundles with pricing

**What happens here:** PlayGeko generates your booking pages, payment links, and membership signup forms. Your marketplace listing goes live so new players can find you.

### Step 3: Invite Players & Go Live (15 minutes)

Your players are waiting. Tell them.

- **Share your booking link:** Players access your booking page through the app or web
- **Import existing members:** Upload a CSV of your current members. They get welcome emails with login details.
- **Set up your branded app:** Connect your app store accounts. Submit for review. Players download your app.
- **Announce leagues and tournaments:** Create your first league. Players join from the app.

**What happens here:** Your courts show up on the PlayGeko marketplace. Players discover you. Bookings start flowing. Revenue starts growing.

## Common Questions

### How long does setup actually take?

Most clubs are fully operational within 48 hours. Simple setups (1-3 courts, basic booking rules) can go live in a few hours. Complex setups (multi-branch, custom integrations) may take 1-2 weeks.

### Do I need technical skills to set up PlayGeko?

No. PlayGeko is designed for club operators, not IT departments. If you can use email and a smartphone, you can set up PlayGeko. The guided setup walks you through every step.

### Can I keep my existing booking system?

You can run both systems in parallel during a transition period. Import your existing members, set up PlayGeko alongside your current system, and switch over when you're ready. Most clubs transition within 2-4 weeks.

### What if I have multiple sports?

PlayGeko handles multi-sport facilities natively. Set up each sport with its own court types, pricing, and scheduling rules. Players see all options in one app.

### Is there a setup fee?

No. PlayGeko has no setup fees, no hidden costs, and no long-term contracts. You pay the monthly subscription and you're live.

### Can I customize the booking experience?

Yes. Set your own booking rules, pricing tiers, cancellation policies, and member benefits. Configure everything from the admin dashboard without touching code.
