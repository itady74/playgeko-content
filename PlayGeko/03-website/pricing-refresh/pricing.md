## Commission Model

| Booking Source | Commission |
|---------------|------------|
| Direct club bookings | 0% |
| Marketplace-originated bookings | 6% |

**What this means:** When a player books directly through your club's app or website, you pay 0% commission. When a player discovers you through the PlayGeko marketplace and books, you pay 6%.

**No surprises:** Commission is only charged on marketplace bookings. No hidden fees. No additional charges.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. PlayGeko offers a free trial so you can test the platform before committing. No credit card required.

### Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

### What payment methods do you accept?

PlayGeko accepts all major credit cards, bank transfers, and local payment methods depending on your region.

### Is there a setup fee?

No. PlayGeko has no setup fees. You're live in 48 hours, not 48 days.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel at any time with 30 days notice.

### What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is exported and available for download for 90 days after cancellation. After that, it's permanently deleted.

### Do I need to buy hardware?

No. PlayGeko works on any device with a web browser — laptop, tablet, or smartphone. No special hardware required.

### How does the marketplace commission work?

When a player discovers your club through the PlayGeko marketplace and books, you pay 6% commission on that booking. Direct bookings through your app or website have 0% commission.
